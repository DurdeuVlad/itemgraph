# Code of Conduct

ItemGraph is a technical project about moderation and forensic tooling. Keep
participation professional, specific, and safe for the people whose data the
project may process.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while challenging technical claims.
- Focus criticism on code, evidence, decisions, and behavior.
- Protect private player, server, and contributor information.
- Share reproducible facts and label hypotheses as hypotheses.
- Accept correction and revise claims when the evidence changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or real moderation evidence without consent.
- Requesting, posting, or committing credentials, tokens, or private server data.
- Deliberately introducing malware, credential theft, data destruction, or
  privacy leaks.
- Misrepresenting inferred movement as observed evidence.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the
DurdeuVlad GitHub profile:
https://github.com/DurdeuVlad

Do not include private player or server data unless it is necessary to explain
the concern; redact it wherever possible.

Maintainers may edit, close, reject, or restrict participation in issues,
pull requests, or other project spaces when needed to protect contributors,
users, or the project.
